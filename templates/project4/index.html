Project 4 – Preference Elicitation

This project designs and implements two interfaces for eliciting a new user's movie preferences (IMDB 5000 Movie Dataset), under a shared Bradley-Terry / Plackett-Luce preference model: Design 1 repeatedly asks you to pick your preferred movie from a pair; Design 2 asks you to rank a set of ten movies from most to least preferred. A full user-study protocol comparing the two (not run, per the assignment) is described in the report below.

Report

Feature representation and justification (Task 1), the Plackett-Luce extension of Bradley-Terry to rankings (Task 2), and the complete user-study design (Task 3).

Enter the study

You will be randomly assigned to one of the two designs, the same way a real participant would be.
