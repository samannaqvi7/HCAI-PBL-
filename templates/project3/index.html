4. Active learning for expert competence discovery

Uncertainty sampling (querying the expert on the least-confident training articles) vs. random sampling, over a budget of {{ metrics.active_learning.budget }} queries. System accuracy stays flat for both at this budget (an honest finding, not a bug), but uncertainty sampling's queried points are 7–10× richer in "deferring would help" signal; random sampling is slightly better for unbiased competence-profile estimation.
